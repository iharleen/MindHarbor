Student Mental Health & Helpline Directory

Verified Helplines

If you or a fellow student are experiencing academic distress, anxiety, or emotional overload, free and confidential support is available 24/7:

National Helplines (India)

Tele-MANAS — National Tele Mental Health Programme Govt of India

Free, confidential, 24/7 multi-language psychological support by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (MoHFW).

Helpline: 14416 or 1800-891-4416 (Toll-Free, 24/7)

Official Portal: telemanas.mohfw.gov.in

KIRAN Mental Health Helpline 24/7 Toll-Free

Govt. of India (MSJE) dedicated 24/7 mental health helpline providing early screening, first-aid, psychological support, and distress mitigation.

Toll-Free Number: 1800-599-0019

Vandrevala Foundation for Mental Health

Free, confidential 24/7 counseling support and crisis intervention across India via phone and WhatsApp.

Helpline: +91 9999 666 555

NIMHANS Psychosocial Support

Premier National Institute of Mental Health & Neurosciences helpline for youth and student psychosocial counsel.

Helpline: 080-46110007

International & Exchange Student Resources (US Only)

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline US & Canada Only

Dial or text 988 within the United States and Canada for free, confidential, 24/7 support.

Crisis Text Line US Only

Text HOME to 741741 (US phone numbers only) to message a volunteer Crisis Counselor.
